Engineering Philosophy

Designed with surgical focus.

Most modern apps are bloated with complex menus, mandatory sign-up walls, and slow load times. We build lightweight, focused tools designed to save you time.

Surgical Single-Purpose

Each utility app solves one routine problem exceptionally well. No bloated multi-menus, no clutter, and no friction.

Modern Native Stack

Built 100% in Kotlin with Jetpack Compose and Material You dynamic theming. Super lightweight and blazing fast.

Privacy By Default

Your personal household schedules stay on your device. We don't harvest analytics, demand logins, or run remote trackers.
