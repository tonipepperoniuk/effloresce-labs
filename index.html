Engineering Philosophy

Designed with surgical focus.

From supermarket aisle routing and zero-waste freezer tracking to 3-minute tech briefings and anti-graveyard notes, we build utilities without unnecessary bloat.

Surgical Single-Purpose

Listi owns frictionless store shopping, Freezi handles zero-waste freezer prep, Stacki distills daily engineering updates, and Notesi keeps active thoughts out of the note graveyard.

100% On-Device Intelligence

Powered by on-device Google ML Kit OCR and local SQLite databases. Shopping lists, freezer inventories, expiry scans, and personal notes stay private on your phone.

Cross-Platform Native Stack

Built with Kotlin Multiplatform, Compose Multiplatform, SQLDelight & Room SQLite, Material 3 dynamic theming, and butter-smooth 60 FPS performance across iOS & Android.
