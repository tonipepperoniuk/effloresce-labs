Engineering Philosophy

Designed with surgical focus.

From supermarket aisle routing to preventing freezer burn, we build utilities that solve real household friction without unnecessary bloat.

Surgical Single-Purpose

Listi focuses purely on frictionless store shopping and price tracking. Freezi focuses purely on freezer management and zero-waste meal prep.

100% On-Device Intelligence

Powered by on-device Google ML Kit OCR and local SQLite databases. All receipt scans, expiration dates, and shopping lists remain private on your phone.

Modern Native Android Stack

Built 100% in Kotlin 2.0 with Jetpack Compose, Material 3 dynamic theming, Glance home screen widgets, and butter-smooth 60 FPS performance.
