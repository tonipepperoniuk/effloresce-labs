Engineered for Frictionless Grocery Shopping

Built for the real supermarket floor.

No messy cloud logins, no slow loading spinners in poor mobile reception areas. Just surgical grocery efficiency.

Smart Aisle Route Sorting

Listi automatically maps items to the exact layout of your local supermarket. Bread, milk, and veg are grouped into an optimal walking order.

Live Shop Mode

Keeps your screen awake, disables accidental navigation gestures, provides tactile haptic feedback on checkoff, and tracks your progress live.

100% Offline & Private

Supermarkets are notorious dead zones for cellular signal. Listi stores all lists in a local SQLite database on your device with zero cloud lag.

Rapid Entry & Voice Dictation

Add multiple items at once by pasting a recipe, speak items aloud via continuous speech recognition, or rapid-fire add with return-key flow.

Custom Store Profiles

Every supermarket is laid out differently. Customize aisle orders for your neighbourhood Tesco Extra, Aldi, or Sainsbury's with simple drag and drop.

Home Screen Widgets

Check off items directly from your Android & iOS home screen widgets without ever needing to launch the app while standing at the shelf.
