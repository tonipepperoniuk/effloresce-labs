Frequently Asked Questions

Why does organizing my Aldi list by aisle save so much time?

Aldi stores use a directional one-way flow starting with Fruit & Veg and ending at Frozen and Checkouts. Walking back against the flow when you forget an item in Aisle 1 wastes minutes. Listi sorts your groceries in Aldi's exact sequence automatically.

Can I customize the aisle order if my local Aldi is laid out differently?

Yes! Inside the Listi app (on iOS and Android), you can drag and drop aisles in the Store Layout Editor to match your exact local Aldi branch.
