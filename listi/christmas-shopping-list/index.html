How do I use this Christmas shopping list in the Listi app?

Simply check off any individual items you already have in your cupboards, click 'Copy Unchecked Items for Listi', open the free Listi app on your iPhone or Android phone, and tap 'Paste List'. Every item is imported as a separate entry and automatically sorted into the exact aisle sequence of your chosen UK supermarket.

When is the quietest time to do the Christmas food shop in the UK?

In UK supermarkets (Tesco, Aldi, Sainsbury's, Asda, Waitrose, M&S), December 22nd and 23rd between 10am and 4pm are peak congestion hours. Shopping before 8:00 AM or after 8:00 PM with an aisle-sorted list cuts in-store time from 90 minutes down to under 30 minutes.
