Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Summary: Effloresce Labs develops native Android utility applications (including Listi and Freezi). We prioritize local, on-device data processing and privacy. Your personal shopping lists, freezer inventories, and scanned data are stored locally on your device.

1. Introduction

Effloresce Labs ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy applies to our website (https://efflorescelabs.com) and our mobile applications distributed on Google Play and other platforms, including Listi, Freezi, and upcoming utilities (collectively, the "Services").

By using our Services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. On-Device Data Storage & Processing

Our core architecture is built on an offline-first foundation:

  • Local Databases: Your items, categories, freezer maps, supermarket aisle layouts, and personal notes are stored strictly in local on-device SQLite databases (AndroidX Room). We do not operate remote servers to harvest or store your personal entries.
  • On-Device Machine Learning & OCR: In apps such as Freezi and Listi, camera optical character recognition (OCR) is performed entirely on-device using Google ML Kit and local heuristics. Camera video frames and scanned images are processed in volatile memory and are not uploaded to the cloud.

3. Device Permissions & Usage

Our applications may request specific Android permissions solely to provide core utility features:

  • Camera (`android.permission.CAMERA`): Used exclusively within scanner features (such as Freezi's expiration date & packaging scanner). Video frames are analyzed locally in real time and never recorded or transmitted remotely.
  • Notification Listener (`NotificationListenerService`): Used optionally in Listi to allow users to automatically parse shopping items received from chat messages. Notification content is parsed locally and discarded immediately after extracting relevant items.
  • Vibration & Wake Lock (`VIBRATE`, `WAKE_LOCK`): Used in in-store shopping modes to keep the screen active while you shop and provide tactile haptic feedback when checking off items.
  • Post Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`): Used to deliver optional reminders, such as items nearing expiration or recurring routine triggers.

4. Third-Party Advertising & Services

To support ongoing development and offer our utility apps to users, our applications may display advertisements served by third-party advertising partners, primarily Google AdMob.

Third-party ad networks may automatically collect and use certain non-personally identifiable information, such as:

  • Advertising Identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID / AAID).
  • Device technical data (device model, operating system version, carrier, network connection type).
  • General diagnostic and performance logs to measure ad delivery and prevent fraudulent activity.

You can control or reset personalized advertising in your device settings by going to Settings > Google > Ads on your Android device. For more details on how Google processes ad data, please review the Google Privacy & Terms for Advertising.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

Our applications utilize reliable third-party libraries:

  • Google Play Services: For app distribution, in-app updates, and licensing. (Privacy Policy)
  • Google ML Kit (On-Device): For local text recognition and barcode scanning. (Terms & Privacy)
  • Google AdMob: For ad delivery and monetization. (AdMob Policy)

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all application records are stored locally on your device:

  • You have complete control over your data at all times.
  • Clearing app data via Settings > Apps > [App Name] > Clear Storage or uninstalling the application permanently deletes all locally stored data from your device.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Services are general utility applications and are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate measures.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this document. We advise you to review this page periodically for any updates.
